## What it is

CRISTAL Lab (Communication, Reasoning, Intelligence & Safety for Trustworthy ALignment) is a new, founding research group at CISPA, a Helmholtz Association institute. It works on LLM alignment, AI safety and robustness, agentic AI and continual learning, and benchmarking and evaluation. The working language is English.

## Who applies

PhD applicants need a master's degree in computer science, data science and AI, or a related field. Postdoc applicants need a PhD and a strong publication record at venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP or COLM. BSc/MSc/PhD interns can apply through a separate page.

## Funding

Full-time paid positions on the TVöD E13 scale, with health insurance, 30 days of paid vacation, a pension scheme, language classes, travel funding and visa support. German is not required.

## How to apply

Apply only through the CISPA portal (emailed applications are not accepted) with one PDF containing your CV, a 2–3 page research statement, up to 3 representative artifacts, transcripts, and 3 references. Optional written answers to the lab's research questions are encouraged. PhD applications get early review from 15 Oct 2026 and priority review until 15 Dec 2026 (rolling). Postdoc and intern positions are open year-round.
