## What it is

MBZUAI is a graduate AI university in Abu Dhabi offering M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs in AI (unified, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, NLP), Computer Science, Robotics, Statistics & Data Science, Computational Biology, and Human-Computer Interaction (Ph.D. only).

## Who applies

Holders of a STEM degree from a UAE-MoE-recognized institution with a minimum CGPA of 3.0 or 3.5 (out of 4.0, depending on program). Ph.D. applicants also need a master's degree transcript, a research statement and three referees (M.Sc.: two).

## Funding

Ph.D. students are centrally funded in Year 1 while matching with a faculty advisor, then move to advisor funding no later than Year 2 (subject to advisor match and progress). M.Sc. funding is not guaranteed; it comes from merit-based funding, a faculty-sponsored assistantship, or a government scholarship for UAE nationals. Funded students get free tuition, a monthly stipend, one yearly return flight, health insurance and a UAE residence visa/Emirates ID.

## How to apply

The portal opened 7 Sept 2026; final deadline is 15 Dec 2026, 5 p.m. GST (Computational Biology: 22 Jan 2027). Steps: online form, CV, statement of purpose (PDF), referees, English proof, screening exam, then a technical interview for a subset. AED 200 fee, waived with a 75%+ exam score. Decisions by 31 Mar 2027; offer reply by 15 Apr 2027.
