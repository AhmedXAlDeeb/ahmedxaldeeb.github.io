## What it is

The Wireless and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (WAI Lab) at Inatel, led by Prof. Felipe A. P. de Figueiredo with Prof. Rausley A. A. de Souza, is seeking M.Sc., Ph.D. and postdoctoral researchers. Topics include computer vision, machine learning, embedded systems and IoT, edge computing, RISC-V neural processors, spectral sensing for cognitive radio, software-defined radio, and fluid/movable antennas and intelligent surfaces.

## Who applies

M.Sc. applicants come from electrical, telecommunications, computer engineering, computer science or IT backgrounds. Ph.D. applicants need a master's degree in a related field. Inatel's M.Sc. program holds a CAPES grade of 5, and over 90% of its students study on scholarships.

## Funding

Tuition is paid by default (about R$1,892–4,188 per month for the M.Sc., depending on the number of courses), but tuition scholarships can cover up to 100%. Stipends from agencies such as CAPES, FAPEMIG, CNPq, FAPESP and xGMobile range from R$2,100–3,500/month (M.Sc.) to R$3,100–6,500/month (Ph.D.). The lab says it may offer supplementary scholarships of R$1,000–4,000/month, research internships, summer schools, and joint-degree options with partners in Canada, France, Germany and Finland.

## How to apply

Email Prof. Figueiredo (felipe.figueiredo@inatel.br) and Prof. Rausley (rausley@inatel.br) first, then apply through Inatel's official process. For the 2027 first-semester intake, applications run until 23 Jan 2027; results come on 3 Feb 2027, scholarship exams on 11 Feb, enrollment on 22–27 Feb, and classes start on 1 Mar 2027. Postdoc and other positions are arranged directly with the lab.
