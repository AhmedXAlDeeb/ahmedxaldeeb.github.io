## What it is

Dr. Iuliia Kotseruba, Assistant Professor in Guelph's School of Computer Science, is recruiting M.Sc. and Ph.D. students for research on vision foundation models for road user behavior understanding, drivers' attention allocation, and more realistic simulated road users. Her earlier work covers pedestrian behavior and driver gaze prediction.

## Who applies

M.Sc. applicants need a four-year honours degree in computer science (or another discipline with a computer science minor) and a 75% average over the last two years. Ph.D. applicants need a thesis-based master's degree, the same average, and a secured advisor before applying. GRE is not required. Her lab looks for strong Python skills; a GPA above 3.2/4, research experience, and computer vision or deep-learning experience make an application stand out.

## Funding

SoCS guarantees at least a $20,000/year stipend (M.Sc., up to 2 years) or $25,000/year (Ph.D.) to admitted students who secure an advisor, paid through teaching and research assistantships. NSERC (domestic) and OGS scholarships are encouraged. Her lab page notes possible top-ups from her grants where they permit.

## How to apply

First fill in [her interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd1fEWXjtoQhXOkCEbR63jxmeM5f60CEfEnYgrKKHTRuS5pcw/viewform) — emails may land in spam, and shortlisted applicants are invited to interview. Then apply online via Guelph indicating her name, for Summer 2027, Fall 2027, or Winter 2028 starts. Official deadlines: Fall 2027 is Mar 1, 2027 (international) or Jun 1, 2027 (domestic); Winter 2028 is Aug 1, 2027 (international) or Oct 1, 2027 (domestic). Required documents: transcripts, CV, a statement of interest, 2 references (M.Sc.) or 3 (Ph.D.), and an English test if applicable (Ph.D.: IELTS 7.0 with 6.5 in each part, or TOEFL 100).
