## What it is

Dr. Lorenzo De Carli (Electrical and Software Engineering, University of Calgary) is looking for a capable PhD student to work on user-centered security and privacy, especially the challenges faced by vulnerable populations. The student joins a Calgary-based multidisciplinary team that includes Prof. Khosro Salmani and Prof. Terrance Mok of Mount Royal University.

## Who applies

An MS in computer science or electrical and computer engineering is expected, along with basic cybersecurity knowledge. Applications that don't discuss your experience, interest or fit with these topics will be removed. Exceptional MS applicants may also be considered. Admission to UCalgary's ECE PhD also asks for a 3.5/4.0 GPA, two references and English proficiency where applicable.

## Funding

Not stated on the flyer. Ask Dr. De Carli about funding before applying.

## How to apply

Submit the online application form before 31 Dec 2026, although a candidate may be selected sooner. Not every application will receive a response. The position starts as soon the selected student is available. Questions can go to lorenzo.decarli@ucalgary.ca.
