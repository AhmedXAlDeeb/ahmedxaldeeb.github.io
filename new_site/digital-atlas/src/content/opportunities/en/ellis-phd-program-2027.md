## What it is

The ELLIS PhD Program is a pan-European meta-program in machine learning and AI. A single central application reaches the advisors listed in the portal. Each student is co-supervised by two advisors in different European countries (Academic Track), by an academic and an industry advisor (Industry Track), or by an ML advisor and a non-AI expert (Interdisciplinary Track), with an exchange of at least 6 months. ELLIS does not award degrees, so you enroll at the host university.

## Who applies

Prospective PhD students in ML and related fields. There is no publication requirement for new applicants, but admission is competitive: in a typical round fewer than 5% of registered applicants are accepted. Local requirements such as GPA, BSc eligibility and language tests vary by institution, and the PhD degree must come from a European institution (with limited exceptions).

## Funding

Funding comes from your advisors' resources, with at least 3 years provided. The nature and amount of funding vary by institution. ELLIS provides only smaller travel and mobility grants.

## How to apply

Register on the ELLIS portal and apply by 31 Oct 2026, 23:59 AoE with a two-page motivational letter/research statement, a CV, unofficial transcripts, and 2–3 referees (requests must be sent before the deadline; referees have until 25 Nov 2026). Choose up to 10 preferred advisors. Some advisors' institutions also require a parallel application with earlier deadlines. Interviews run Jan–Feb 2027 and decisions come late Feb–Mar 2027.
