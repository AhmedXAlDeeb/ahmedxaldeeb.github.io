## What it is

A competitive University of Auckland scheme with four rounds each year. It supports doctoral candidates whose research fits the university's and the relevant faculty's strategic objectives. The number of awards varies, and each faculty publishes its own selection criteria.

## Who applies

Domestic students and international students who are eligible to pay domestic fees, which in practice means PhD applicants only. You must not already hold a doctorate or have previously received this scholarship. PhD entry normally requires a master's or an honours degree at about a B+ level.

## Funding

A tax-free stipend of NZ$35,468 per year (latest confirmed rate, 2026) for up to 42 months full-time, plus tuition and the Student Services Fee. For comparison, the stipend was NZ$34,569 in 2025 and NZ$33,825 in 2024. The 2027 amount isn't published yet and is adjusted each January for inflation. International students also get the compulsory health insurance charge. Paid work is capped at 750 hours per 12 months.

## How to apply

There is no separate scholarship application. Apply for your doctoral programme by 1 Nov 2026 and indicate that you want scholarship consideration. Most faculties require a confirmed supervisor who endorses your one-page Statement of Research Intent, and some need supervisor statements by the closing date. Offers come by 1 Feb 2027, and international enrolment is between 1 Apr and 1 Sep 2027. Later rounds close 1 Feb, 1 May and 1 Aug 2027. The closing time isn't published, so submit early.
