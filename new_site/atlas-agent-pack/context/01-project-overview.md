# 01 — Project Overview (v2)

## What
**The Digital Atlas** is Ahmed Soliman's personal site with three jobs, in priority order:
1. **Tell his story and background** — briefly, credibly, humanly (builds trust).
2. **Sell the paid personal mentorship**: helping CS and Engineering students land **fully funded MSc and PhD opportunities in Canada** (more countries later).
3. **Publish articles and sessions** on the same topics (builds audience and search traffic; feeds the mentorship).

Secondary: a compact portfolio/projects section as proof of technical credibility (AI, computer vision, biomedical engineering).

## Primary audience
CS and Engineering students and graduates (internationally) who want funded graduate study in Canada and need structure: English test, finding a supervisor, applications, documents, visa.
Secondary: employers, supervisors, collaborators who find the site through his work.

## What visitors should feel within seconds
"This person has walked this path, explains it clearly, and can guide me step by step."

## Tone
Warm, direct, credible, calm. Minimalist. A journey metaphor: **a small traveler walking across the Earth** — from where you are to where you want to study.

## Signature
A minimalist Earth horizon with an illustrated figure walking on its surface; scrolling advances the journey (see `context/04`). Keep it simple, beautiful, and light.

## Conversion goals
- Primary CTA: **Book a free intro call** (mentorship)
- Secondary CTAs: **Read my story**, **Read the guides**, **Join a session**

## Success criteria
1. Story and mentorship value are clear in the first screen and first scroll.
2. Offer page explains stages, deliverables, pricing, and next step with no ambiguity.
3. Claims are honest and sourced; no guarantees.
4. Earth-walker scene is memorable yet minimal, fast, and optional (full fallback).
5. Articles and sessions are easy to publish and find (tags, search).
6. Accessible, responsive, deploys reliably on GitHub Pages.
