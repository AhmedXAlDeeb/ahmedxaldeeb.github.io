# 06 — Mentorship Offer and Persuasive Copy

## Offer in one line
Personal 1:1 mentorship that guides CS and Engineering students from **IELTS to a funded MSc/PhD offer in Canada**, with a weekly meeting, a defined timeline and plan, tasks, and detailed feedback. (Other countries later: show as "Coming soon", no promises.)

## Who it's for / not for
**For:** CS/Engineering (incl. AI, software, electrical, biomedical, mechanical…) students and graduates aiming at thesis-based MSc or PhD with funding in Canada, willing to do weekly work.
**Not for:** anyone seeking guaranteed admission/funding, ghost-written documents, or visa "shortcuts". State this plainly; it builds trust.

## The six stages (the "path" and the Earth pins)
1. **Foundation & English test (IELTS):** target scores, study plan, test strategy, booking timeline.
2. **Profile & roadmap:** profile audit, target universities/programs, funding routes, timeline backwards from deadlines.
3. **Supervisor search & outreach:** finding aligned professors, reading their work, writing cold emails, follow-ups, interviews.
4. **Applications:** program requirements, application portals, references, deadlines tracking.
5. **Documents:** CV (academic/research), **Statement of Purpose**, **cover letters**, research proposal if needed — reviewed with detailed feedback and revisions.
6. **Visa & landing:** study-permit application checklist and review, financial/document preparation, pre-departure planning. (All rules → official IRCC/university sources with last-verified dates; mentor is not an immigration lawyer/consultant.)

## What a mentee gets
- Weekly 1:1 call (45 min), agenda and notes after each call
- A written **plan and timeline** with milestones, updated monthly
- **Tasks each week** with detailed written feedback (target response ≤ 48h)
- Document reviews (CV, SOP, cover letters, outreach emails) with tracked revisions
- Templates, checklists, and a progress tracker
- Private message channel (scope depends on tier)

## Persuasion principles (honest persuasion)
- Lead with the student's goal and fear (cost, uncertainty, confusing process), then show a clear path and Ahmed's real experience.
- **Specific over hype:** name stages, deliverables, cadence, response times.
- **Proof, real only:** Ahmed's own journey, mentee results with permission, verifiable credentials. Until real proof exists, omit the section or label "first cohort".
- **Honest expectations:** "I can't guarantee admission or funding. I can make your application stronger, your plan clearer, and your timeline realistic."
- **Reduce risk:** free intro call, written scope, clear policies.
- **Clear next step:** one primary CTA repeated (Book a free intro call).
- Avoid: fake scarcity, invented testimonials, inflated numbers, "guaranteed", "100% success", pressure tactics, university logos without permission.
